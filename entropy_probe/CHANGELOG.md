## Diagnosis note (2026-07-21)
The μKV-1B GPU prefill "regression" (191 s vs table's 117 s) was **NOT** a stale
binary — it was a transient vendor thermal-daemon capping the GPU to **726 MHz**
(vs the table's 1200 MHz). Clock-normalized: 117 × 1200/726 = 193 s ≈ measured
191 s. The prefill fix works.

**Follow-up validation (2026-07-21):** a cold, held-1200-MHz run produced
123.4 s prefill (all GPU-active prefill samples at 1200 MHz), confirming the
clock explanation to within 5%. It also produced 44.0 tok/s decode vs the
07-15 artifact's 31.9 tok/s under matching visible metadata. Therefore the
two rows must **not** be treated as whole-row reproductions: the decode delta
is unresolved because the historical Vulkan binary was not preserved and the
07-17 source edit was untracked. See `VALIDATION_GPU1B_1200_2026-07-21.md`.
